Cold Turkey for Mac and Mobile: Closing the Cross-Device Gap

By NowFocus team Published 3 October 2026 7 min read

Cold Turkey Blocker is rightfully celebrated in the productivity community. Developed by Felix Belzile, it earned legendary status by doing what other software was too timid to attempt: enforcing real, unbypassable locks on desktop operating systems. If you locked Reddit for three hours on Cold Turkey, you could restart your computer, try killing background tasks, and fiddle with your system clock, yet Reddit remained locked.

However, modern attention is rarely confined to a single screen. When knowledge workers lock their Mac or Windows PC using Cold Turkey, an insidious behavioral pattern unfolds: their hand unconsciously reaches into their pocket, pulls out their smartphone, and spends the next 45 minutes scrolling through Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts.

Cold Turkey solved the desktop equation, but completely abandoned mobile. If you are searching for Cold Turkey on Mac and mobile, here is what you need to know about the multi-device focus gap and how NowFocus solves it.

Verdict at a Glance

The Reality of Cold Turkey Across Devices

Cold Turkey Blocker excels at: Ironclad, unbypassable desktop lockdowns on Windows and macOS with one-time purchase pricing.

The fatal limitation: Cold Turkey has no mobile app (Android was discontinued years ago and iOS was never built). Locking your laptop while your phone is wide open leaves your attention completely defenseless.

Why choose NowFocus: NowFocus bridges desktop rigor (macOS and Windows) with native mobile discipline (Android early-access build available, iOS planned), bringing unbypassable locks, progressive friction, and selective feed blocking across your entire personal ecosystem.

1. The "Smartphone Escape Hatch" problem

Distraction is fluid. It flows along the path of least cognitive resistance. When you are writing a research paper, analyzing code, or reviewing contracts, mental resistance is inevitable. When Cold Turkey blocks your desktop web browser, your brain immediately redirects that dopamine deficit to the pocket supercomputer next to your keyboard.

Because Cold Turkey discontinued its mobile Android application years ago and never supported iPhone, users are forced to stitch together fragmented solutions: Cold Turkey on desktop, a separate Screen Time pin on iOS, or a buggy third-party app on Android. The moment one device is less protected than the other, your focus routine disintegrates.

NowFocus is designed from first principles as an intentional multi-device environment manager. By aligning the blocking philosophy across mobile and desktop, your digital boundaries remain consistent regardless of which screen is in front of you.

2. Surgical feed blocking vs. nuclear shutdowns

Cold Turkey is a blunt instrument. It blocks domains or executables entirely. While this is effective for purely unproductive sites like online casinos or gaming hubs, modern knowledge work often requires nuanced boundaries:

  • You need YouTube to watch a 40-minute programming tutorial, but you cannot afford to get sucked into an infinite spiral of 30-second YouTube Shorts.
  • You need LinkedIn to reply to an inbound hiring candidate, but you don't want to browse the algorithmic newsfeed.
  • You need your smartphone nearby for two-factor authentication codes and urgent family calls, but opening the screen shouldn't reveal notification badges.

NowFocus addresses this modern nuance directly. On Android, NowFocus features experimental partial blocking that surgically hides YouTube Shorts, the algorithmic Home feed, and Instagram/Facebook Reels while allowing productive long-form videos and essential messaging to function normally.

3. Calibrated exit friction: From reflex to reflection

Cold Turkey made its reputation on the "Frozen Turkey" feature, which locks you completely out of your computer. While powerful for extreme deadlines, all-or-nothing extremes often lead users to avoid starting a block session in the first place because they fear being locked out if an urgent matter arises.

NowFocus bridges the gap with three distinct modes:

  • Normal mode: Prompts you for intentional confirmation before ending a session.
  • Strict mode: Requires typing a commitment sentence, waiting through an unskippable 30-second timer, and listening to your own 10-second voice memo. This 30-second delay is specifically calibrated to allow emotional dopamine cravings to dissipate.
  • Locked mode: Enforces non-negotiable blocking until the countdown reaches zero, honoring Cold Turkey's classic philosophy.

The 14-Day Commitment Shield

For recurring temptations like adult sites, gambling, and addictive social platforms, temporary sessions are insufficient. NowFocus includes a 14-Day Commitment Shield. Once activated, you have a 60-second undo window. After that window closes, the rule is locked for two weeks straight on your device. It provides the permanent lockdown Cold Turkey users love, without abandoning mobile.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an official Cold Turkey app for Android or iPhone?

No. Cold Turkey mobile was discontinued years ago and only desktop versions for Windows and macOS exist. The developer has stated that mobile operating system constraints prevent them from offering their signature unbypassable architecture.

How does NowFocus stop phone distractions without breaking calls?

NowFocus lets you block specific apps, websites, and addictive feeds like Shorts while keeping essential contacts and communication reachable. You can whitelist phone calls, emergency SMS, and productive utilities while locking down doomscrolling.

How hard is it to bypass NowFocus compared to Cold Turkey?

Cold Turkey is renowned for completely unbypassable desktop blocks. NowFocus brings this same ironclad philosophy to Locked mode, but also adds a Strict mode where exit requires typing a commitment phrase, waiting 30 seconds, and hearing your voice note.
