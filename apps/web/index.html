01 · What is NowFocus?

A focus system, not a blocklist.

NowFocus is a deep-work app for Android, macOS and Windows: start a session and the apps and sites you chose go dark until the timer ends. It runs on your device, so what you block and what you try to open never reaches us.

  1. 01

    Start a session

    Pick a Focus Profile, the apps and sites you want out of the way, and a length.

  2. 02

    Distractions go dark

    Blocked apps are covered by a full-screen message, and blocked sites simply won’t load. On Android and macOS the message is a calm “This can wait.” with a one-tap call or text to someone who matters, or a reminder of a goal you set.

  3. 03

    Leaving costs something

    Choose how hard quitting is: any time, Strict (type a short sentence, then wait 30 seconds), or Locked, with no early exit until the timer ends.

02 · Why it’s different

Most blockers ask you to be strong. This one doesn’t.

A

Friction, not willpower

Quitting early is possible in Strict mode, but slow and deliberate: type a sentence, then wait 30 seconds. You’re reminded of a goal you set, and on Android and macOS you also hear a 10-second note you recorded to yourself. It becomes a decision, not a reflex.

Strict mode · Android, macOS and Windows (in development)

B

Blocks the feed, not the app

Keep YouTube, lose the Shorts. Experimental partial blocking hides Shorts, Reels and recommendation lists while the rest of the app keeps working.

Android, experimental · YouTube Shorts, Home feed and Up next; Facebook Reels

C

Private by design

Your stats are counted on your device and we never see what you browse. There is no account to create, and blocking never waits on a server.

Local-first · works offline
